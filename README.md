# Salesforce Admissions & Student Performance Automation

## Project Overview
This portfolio case study demonstrates my experience as a Business Analyst delivering a Salesforce solution to improve student admissions processing and academic performance monitoring.

**Role:** Business Analyst / Salesforce Configuration  
**Project Type:** Client Project  
**Project Lifecycle:** Requirements Gathering → Solution Design → Salesforce Configuration → UAT → Deployment

## Business Problem
The existing admissions process involved manual routing and processing delays.

The organisation also needed improved visibility into changes in student academic performance.

## Business Objectives
- Streamline admissions processing.
- Automate routing of submitted admission forms.
- Identify students whose grades declined by 25% or more.
- Improve reporting and decision-making.

## My Responsibilities
- Gathered and analysed business requirements.
- Documented business processes and solution requirements.
- Designed and configured Salesforce Flows.
- Created reports and dashboards.
- Participated in UAT and supported deployment.

## Salesforce Solution
**Admissions Automation:** Salesforce Flow automatically routed submitted forms to the appropriate team.

**Student Performance Monitoring:** Automation compared previous and current grades and flagged significant declines.

**Reporting:** Salesforce reports and dashboards provided teachers and directors with visibility into student performance.

## Business Outcome
The solution contributed to reducing admissions processing time from approximately three weeks to five days.
