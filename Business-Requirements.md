# Business Requirements Document (BRD)
## Salesforce Admissions & Student Performance Automation

**Project:** Education Sector Admissions Automation  
**Role:** Business Analyst  
**Document Type:** Illustrative Portfolio BRD  
**Platform:** Salesforce CRM

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The project aimed to improve admissions processing efficiency and student performance monitoring through Salesforce automation.

The admissions process previously took approximately three weeks, creating operational delays. The solution introduced automated application routing, academic performance monitoring and reporting.

## 2. Business Problem

The existing process presented the following challenges:

- Manual admissions routing.
- Delays in processing applications.
- Limited automated identification of declining academic performance.
- Need for better management reporting.

## 3. Business Objectives

- Reduce admissions processing time.
- Route submitted applications to the appropriate teams.
- Automatically identify student grade declines of 25% or more.
- Improve visibility into academic performance through Salesforce dashboards.

## 4. Stakeholders

Illustrative stakeholder roles:

- Admissions Team
- Academic Staff
- Department Directors
- Salesforce Administrator
- Business Analyst

## 5. Business Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| BR-001 | The system should route submitted admissions forms to the appropriate team. | High |
| BR-002 | The system should compare current and previous student grades. | High |
| BR-003 | The system should flag performance declines of 25% or more. | High |
| BR-004 | The system should provide academic performance reports and dashboards. | High |
| BR-005 | Relevant users should be able to review admissions and performance information. | Medium |

## 6. Proposed Solution

Salesforce automation will support:

- Admissions form routing using Salesforce Flow.
- Student grade comparison and performance flagging.
- Reporting and dashboards for academic stakeholders.

## 7. Acceptance Criteria

- Submitted admissions forms are routed according to the configured routing rules.
- Grade comparisons identify declines meeting the defined 25% threshold.
- Reports display relevant student performance information.
- Authorised stakeholders can access the appropriate dashboards.

## 8. Testing and Deployment

The project included configuration, testing, UAT and deployment activities.

Detailed sample UAT test cases will be documented separately.

## 9. Business Outcome

The delivered solution contributed to reducing admissions processing time from approximately three weeks to five days.
